OUR HERITAGE

Our Story & Passion for Coffee

At Coffee & Cravings, we believe that great coffee and wholesome food are more than just a treat—they are an experience, a moment to savor, and a beautiful way to connect with people in Patna.

Our journey began with a simple passion: to bring genuine third-wave artisan coffee to Mithapur, Patna. Sourcing 100% Arabica estate beans, roasting them with precision, and serving velvety cappuccinos, bold cold brews, and creamy authentic Italian pastas crafted from scratch.

Whether you are catching up with close friends, going on an intimate date, or working remotely on your laptop with our high-speed Wi-Fi, Coffee & Cravings welcomes you like home.
