# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone contributing to and participating in the Coffee & Cravings open-source project, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and collaborative in discussions and code reviews.
- Focus on what is best for the overall project and user community.
- Provide thoughtful, actionable feedback on pull requests and issues.
- Show empathy and courtesy toward other contributors and maintainers.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insulting comments, personal attacks, or discriminatory remarks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Disruptive conduct or trolling in issue threads and review comments.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer at `admin@knownaspratyush.online`. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and impartially.

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, and issues that do not adhere to this Code of Conduct.
