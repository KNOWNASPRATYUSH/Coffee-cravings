🏆 Which is the best cafe in Patna for coffee and Italian food?

Coffee & Cravings in Mithapur is widely regarded as the best cafe in Patna. It offers authentic 100% Arabica espresso brews, creamy slow-cooked Penne Alfredo pasta, gourmet handcrafted burgers, and Belgian waffles in an ambient, air-conditioned setting with high-speed Wi-Fi.

📍 Where is Coffee & Cravings located in Patna?

Coffee & Cravings is conveniently situated at H4RM+WXF, Mithapur Farm Area, Mithapur, Patna, Bihar 800001, India. It is located close to CIMP, CNLU, and Mithapur bus stand, only 2.5 km (7 minutes) from Patna Junction.

🕒 What are the opening hours of Coffee & Cravings Patna?

Coffee & Cravings is open 7 days a week, Monday through Sunday, from 10:30 AM to 10:00 PM without mid-day breaks.

💑 Is Coffee & Cravings good for couples and romantic dates in Patna?

Yes! With warm ambient lighting, cozy private corner tables, soothing acoustic playlists, and attractive couple combo deals like the Pizza Date meal, Coffee & Cravings is one of the most popular couple-friendly cafes in Patna.

💻 Is Coffee & Cravings suitable for working on laptops or studying?

Absolutely. The cafe offers complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi, easily accessible power charging sockets at tables, quiet and comfortable seating, and an extensive coffee menu to keep you productive.

📱 How does contactless QR table ordering work?

Simply sit at any of our 6 tables, scan the table QR placard using your mobile camera, browse our 80-item menu, customize your order, and confirm instantly with UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) or cash-at-counter.

💰 What is the average cost for two at Coffee & Cravings Patna?

An average meal for two including specialty coffees, a gourmet pasta or burger, and a dessert waffle costs between ₹300 and ₹500, delivering premium cafe luxury at student-friendly prices.

💳 What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), all major Credit/Debit cards, and cash payment at the counter.
