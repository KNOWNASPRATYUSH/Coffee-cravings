Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026 • Coffee & Cravings, Patna

Coffee & Cravings ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is dedicated to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and applicable data protection regulations. This Privacy Policy informs you how we collect, handle, and safeguard your data when you visit our website, dine with us, or place an order.

🔒 Our Privacy Commitment:

We collect only the bare minimum data needed to prepare your artisan food, generate digital receipts, and verify dining tables. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third-party advertising brokers.

1. Personal Data We Collect

To serve you at our cafe and fulfill your orders, we collect:

  • Identity & Contact: Name, phone number, and email address (provided via Google Sign-In, 6-digit email OTP, or guest checkout).
  • Order & Dining Details: Table number, dine-in/takeaway choice, items ordered, dietary/allergen notes, and order timestamps.
  • Payment References: Bank transaction / UTR reference numbers generated by certified UPI gateways and payment aggregators. (Note: We NEVER handle, collect, or store your debit/credit card CVV or bank PIN numbers.)
  • Customer Communications: Inquiries, feedback, or messages sent via our contact forms or direct emails.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Your information is processed strictly for legitimate dining and ordering purposes:

  • Preparing and kitchen-dispatching handcrafted food and drinks accurately to your designated table or counter.
  • Generating digital bills, tax invoices, and order status tracking.
  • Sending one-time sign-in verification codes (OTPs) and transactional alerts.
  • Providing prompt customer care and addressing feedback.
  • Delivering optional loyalty rewards (e.g. welcome discount coupons) if you choose to participate.

3. Your Privacy Rights

You retain full ownership and rights over your personal data:

  • Right to Access: View your active receipts, profile, and lifetime order history anytime under "My Orders".
  • Right to Correction: Update inaccurate contact information directly via account settings.
  • Right to Erasure: Permanently delete your account, contact profile, and saved order records at any time using the "Delete Account & Order History" option or by emailing our Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Submit any inquiry, concern, or complaint directly to our designated officer.

4. Data Retention & Security Standards

  • Active kitchen queue tickets automatically expire and are purged after 24 hours.
  • Login OTPs are cryptographically hashed using SHA-256 and purged automatically after 5 minutes.
  • Session cookies are protected with encrypted, HTTP-only, and SameSite strict attributes to prevent cross-site leakage.
