# Accessibility Statement

Coffee & Cravings is dedicated to ensuring digital accessibility for all users, including individuals with disabilities. We continually enhance the user experience and apply relevant accessibility standards.

## Measures to Support Accessibility

- **Semantic HTML5:** Using structured landmarks (`<header>`, `<main>`, `<nav>`, `<aside>`, `<section>`).
- **High Contrast:** Applying readable color ratios across text, buttons, and backgrounds.
- **Keyboard Navigation:** Ensuring clear interactive focus states and logical tab orders.
- **Responsive Layouts:** Designing flexible viewports that accommodate zoom and text scaling up to 200%.
- **Screen Reader Support:** Providing descriptive `alt` tags on images and appropriate ARIA attributes for dynamic dialogs and cart drawers.
