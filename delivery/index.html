Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: May 2026 • Coffee & Cravings, Patna

This Shipping & Delivery Policy outlines the delivery, takeaway, and order fulfillment procedures for Coffee & Cravings (operated in Mithapur Farm Area, Patna, Bihar).

🛵 Service Summary:

We offer Instant Table Service (Dine-in), Takeaway / Curbside Pickup, and Local Cafe Delivery across Mithapur and surrounding neighborhoods in Patna. Typical preparation time is 15 to 25 minutes.

1. Dine-In & Table Delivery

  • How it works: Guests scan the QR code located on their table, select their items, and submit the order.
  • Delivery Time: Orders are freshly prepared and served directly to your designated table number within 10 to 20 minutes depending on kitchen rush.

2. Takeaway & Self-Pickup Orders

  • Order Placement: You can place orders online through our website or by phone for takeaway.
  • Preparation Time: 15 to 25 minutes from receipt of order confirmation.
  • Pickup Location: Coffee & Cravings counter, Mithapur Farm Area, Patna.
  • Packaging: All food and hot/cold beverages are securely packaged in food-grade, leak-proof, temperature-retaining containers with beverage cup holders.

3. Local Home & Office Delivery

  • Delivery Coverage: We deliver to locations in Mithapur, Kankarbagh, Chiraiyatand, Patna Junction vicinity, and nearby areas within a 3–5 km radius of the cafe.
  • Estimated Delivery Time: 35 to 50 minutes (inclusive of food preparation and transit). During peak rush or severe weather, delivery times may vary slightly.
  • Delivery Charges: Applicable delivery charges (if any) are displayed transparently at checkout before payment confirmation.

4. Order Inspection upon Handoff

We advise customers to verify their takeaway or delivery package at the moment of handoff. In the rare event of missing items or spilled drinks, please notify the delivery executive or call us immediately at 620-567-7925 for a prompt resolution.
