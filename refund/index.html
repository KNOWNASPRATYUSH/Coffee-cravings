Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: May 2026 • Coffee & Cravings, Patna

At Coffee & Cravings, we take pride in serving handcrafted coffees, artisan pastas, fresh burgers, and Belgian waffles made to order. We strive to provide an exceptional dining and takeaway experience. This policy explains our terms regarding cancellations, refunds, and replacements.

⚡ Quick Summary:

Because all food and beverages are prepared fresh to order, cancellations can only be accepted before our kitchen begins cooking or brewing. Approved refunds are credited back to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

1. Order Cancellation Policy

Dine-In & QR Table Orders:

  • Once an order is submitted via your table QR code, it is immediately routed to our barista and kitchen display system.
  • You may cancel or amend an order only if you notify the floor staff or counter barista immediately (within 1 to 2 minutes of placing the order), before ticket preparation starts.
  • Once preparation of food or brewing of beverages has begun, cancellations cannot be accepted.

Takeaway & Pre-Orders:

  • Takeaway orders can be cancelled by calling our cafe at 620-567-7925 prior to kitchen preparation.
  • If an order is not picked up within 45 minutes of the designated ready time, no refund can be granted as the item was freshly prepared and held for you.

2. Damaged, Incorrect, or Quality Issues

We stand by the quality of every meal we serve. If you receive an order that is:

  • Incorrect Item: Different from what was ordered on your receipt.
  • Quality Issue: Food prepared incorrectly or temperature discrepancy.
  • Spillage or Damage: Takeaway package compromised upon handoff.

Please inform our floor staff or contact us immediately. We will promptly offer you an immediate fresh remake at no extra charge, or an instant full refund for the affected item.

3. Refund Processing & Timelines

When a refund is approved by our management:

  • UPI & Net Banking: Refunds are initiated through our certified payment gateway (Razorpay) and are credited back to your bank account within 5 to 7 business days, subject to your bank's clearance cycle.
  • Credit / Debit Cards: Reflected in your card statement within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Cash Payments at Counter: Refunded immediately in cash by the counter manager after verifying the bill.

4. Failed Transactions & Double Debits

If your bank account or UPI app was debited but the website showed a payment failure or did not generate an order confirmation:

  • The transaction has not reached our merchant account and will be automatically reversed to your bank account by your UPI app/bank within 2 to 4 working days per NPCI guidelines.
  • If you still do not receive the credit after 4 working days, send your bank UTR / Reference ID to founder@coffeeandcravings.in and we will assist in resolving it with our payment gateway partner.
